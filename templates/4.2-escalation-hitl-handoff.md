# Escalation and Human-in-the-Loop Handoff Specification (HITL)

**Id:** `4.2` · **Prefix:** `HITL` · **Stage:** 4 — Behavior, handoff, and policy · **Version:** `v0.1-DRAFT`

## Normative

### Purpose
Escalation and Human-in-the-Loop Handoff Specification (HITL) defines when an agent run pauses for human intervention and how execution resumes afterward. HITL extends the ABS stop and escalation rules with the handover information, human decision points, resume conditions, timeouts, and audit trail required for that intervention.

### Required sections
1. Human decision points: the human decision points of this HITL version and the corresponding actions.
2. Escalation triggers: the ABS stop and escalation rules that apply to the AA human-in-the-loop for this HITL version.
3. Handover procedure: the payload, channel, and form passed to the human in this HITL version, and the logging mechanisms for those escalations.
4. Resume and continue: the rules to resume and continue execution based on human input for this HITL version, including the timeouts.

### Acceptance gate
The TLA+ of the current version of the Spec is revised to incorporate this HITL version's handover as wait, human decision, and resume, including timeout, and PASS. After recording PASS, the process moves on to the next artifact.

### Boundary
Operable handoff mechanics for human checkpoints.
