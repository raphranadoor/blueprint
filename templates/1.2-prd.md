# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Id:** `1.2` · **Stage:** 1 — Problem Definition · **Version:** `v0.1-DRAFT`

## Normative

### Purpose
Commit to a product design concept. Translate PD into goals, personas, workflows, features, FR/NFR, assumptions/dependencies, and success metrics. Map each product goal to PD outcomes.

### Required sections
1. Product overview
2. Organizational commitment (fund, scope, accept)
3. Goals and objectives (mapped to PD)
4. User personas
5. User workflows
6. Features
7. Functional requirements (FR)
8. Non-functional requirements (NFR)
9. Assumptions and dependencies
10. Success metrics — YES/NO questions with binary answers
11. Out-of-scope

### Acceptance gate
All answers must be YES to approve:
1. Product description, requirements, and organizational commitment stated?
2. Goals stated as desired outcomes?
3. Personas named?
4. Workflows described at user level?
5. Success metrics are YES/NO with binary answers?
6. Every product goal maps to ≥1 PD problem/outcome?
7. Both FR and NFR stated?
8. Out-of-scope defined explicitly?

### Boundary
Product intent and requirements.

### Bridge
→ System Requirements Specification (SRS).
