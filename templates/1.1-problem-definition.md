# Problem Definition (PD)

**Id:** `1.1` · **Prefix:** `PD` · **Stage:** 1 — Problem and requirements · **Version:** `v0.1-DRAFT`

## Normative

### Purpose
Problem Definition (PD) states the problem to be solved, the background required to understand it, and situations in which that problem blocks the user's progress. PD is the first artifact of the Blueprint, and Stage 1 proceeds from a version that has passed PD-C1 through PD-C5.

### Required sections
1. Problem statement: the current problem and the organizational or team setting in which it occurs.
2. Barrier use cases: instances in which the problem blocks the user's progress in the project or in development.
3. Users: primary users who experience the problem, and secondary users where they are relevant.
4. Desired outcomes: solution-neutral end-states for what should be better.
5. Existing solutions gap: what already exists and why it is insufficient for this problem.
6. Out of scope: the bounds of this problem.

### Acceptance gate
All answers must be YES to approve (R1):
1. PD-C1  Is the problem specified independently of a decision to build an agent, a system, or a product?
2. PD-C2  Are the users who experience the problem named, including who is blocked or harmed by it?
3. PD-C3  Is the organizational or team setting stated, and is the problem plausibly solvable with available resources?
4. PD-C4  Are the desired outcomes specified as solution-neutral end-states for what should be better?
5. PD-C5  Are the bounds of this problem specified?

### Boundary
Understand the problem without presupposing a solution.
